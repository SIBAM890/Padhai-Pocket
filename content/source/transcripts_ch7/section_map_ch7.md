## 2. Hero Concept Progression & Chunk Division

Similar to Chapter 5 (`distance_vs_displacement` at S02), Chapter 7 features **`mass_vs_weight` (S05)** and **`universal_gravitation` (S02)** as premier diagnostic learning targets:

### Proposed Hero Concept: `mass_vs_weight` (Sections 7.3 & 7.4, Pages 100–101)
Three sequential pedagogical chunks matching Padhai Pocket engine architecture:
1. `BSE9-PHYS-GRAV-S05-01` (Definition): **ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ଓ ଏହାର ବୈଶିଷ୍ଟ୍ୟ (Definition and Properties of Mass)**
   - Mass is the quantity of matter in an object.
   - It is a measure of inertia.
   - It remains constant everywhere (Earth, Moon, or deep space).
   - SI unit is kilogram (kg); it is a scalar quantity.
2. `BSE9-PHYS-GRAV-S05-02` (Definition): **ଓଜନ ଏବଂ ଏହାର ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନଶୀଳତା (Definition and Variations of Weight)**
   - Weight is the gravitational pull of the Earth on an object: $W = m \times g$.
   - Since $g$ changes with location, weight changes with location.
   - SI unit is Newton (N); it is a vector quantity directed towards Earth's center.
3. `BSE9-PHYS-GRAV-S05-03` (Worked Example & Moon Comparison): **ଚନ୍ଦ୍ରପୃଷ୍ଠରେ ଓଜନ ଓ ଗାଣିତିକ ଉଦାହରଣ (Weight on Moon: 1/6th Ratio)**
   - Since Moon's mass and radius are smaller, $g_m \approx \frac{1}{6} g_e$.
   - A person with mass 60 kg has weight $60 \times 9.8 = 588\text{ N}$ on Earth, but only $\approx 98\text{ N}$ on the Moon, while their mass remains exactly 60 kg!
