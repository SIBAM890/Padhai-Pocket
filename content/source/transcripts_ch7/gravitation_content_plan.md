## 2. Target Diagnostic Concepts & Misconception Taxonomy

In secondary school physics across Odisha, Chapter 7 contains two primary cognitive sticking points:

### Sticking Point 1: `mass_vs_weight` (Primary Hero Candidate)
- **Common Indian/Odia Colloquial Confusion**:
  - In daily spoken Odia, people ask "ତୁମର ଓଜନ କେତେ?" (What is your weight?) and answer "50 କିଲୋଗ୍ରାମ" (50 kg).
  - Students confuse the **property of matter (Mass / ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ in kg)** with the **gravitational force (Weight / ଓଜନ in N)**.
- **Underlying Misconceptions**:
  1. *Misconception M1*: "Weight and mass are the exact same thing."
  2. *Misconception M2*: "If an astronaut goes to the Moon, their body loses mass / they become smaller."
  3. *Misconception M3*: "Objects have no weight in a vacuum."
  4. *Misconception M4*: "Heavier objects accelerate faster towards Earth than lighter objects."

### Sticking Point 2: `universal_gravitation` (Secondary Hero Candidate)
- **Underlying Misconceptions**:
  1. *Misconception G1*: "Gravity only exists on Earth; there is no gravity in space or between two small objects."
  2. *Misconception G2 (Inverse Square Gap)*: "If the distance between two planets is doubled, the force becomes half" (forgetting $d^2 \implies \frac{1}{4}$th).
  3. *Misconception G3*: "The Earth pulls the apple with a huge force, but the apple does not pull the Earth."

## 3. Hero Chunks Division for `mass_vs_weight`

### Chunk 1: `BSE9-PHYS-GRAV-S05-01`
- **Title (Odia)**: ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ଏବଂ ଏହାର ଅପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନଶୀଳତା (Mass and its Invariance)
- **Concept ID**: `mass_vs_weight`
- **Source Page**: 100
- **Verbatim Odia Principle**:
  > ବସ୍ତୁଟିଏ ଯେଉଁ ପରିମାଣର ପଦାର୍ଥ ଧାରଣ କରିଥାଏ, ତାହାକୁ ବସ୍ତୁର ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ (mass) କୁହାଯାଏ । ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ଏକ ଅଦିଶ ରାଶି ଅଟେ ଏବଂ ଏହାର SI ଏକକ କିଲୋଗ୍ରାମ (kg) । ବସ୍ତୁଟି ପୃଥିବୀରେ ଥାଉ, ଚନ୍ଦ୍ରପୃଷ୍ଠରେ ଥାଉ କିମ୍ବା ମହାକାଶରେ ଥାଉ, ଏହାର ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ସର୍ବଦା ଅପରିବର୍ତ୍ତିତ ରହେ । ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ହେଉଛି ବସ୍ତୁର ଜଡ଼ତାର ଏକ ପରିମାପ ।
- **Glossary Terms**: `T_MASS` (ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ)

### Chunk 2: `BSE9-PHYS-GRAV-S05-02`
- **Title (Odia)**: ଓଜନ ଏବଂ ଏହାର ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନଶୀଳତା (Weight and its Variation)
- **Concept ID**: `mass_vs_weight`
- **Source Page**: 100–101
- **Verbatim Odia Principle**:
  > ପୃଥିବୀ ଏକ ବସ୍ତୁକୁ ଯେଉଁ ମାଧ୍ୟାକର୍ଷଣ ବଳ ଦ୍ୱାରା ନିଜ କେନ୍ଦ୍ର ଆଡ଼କୁ ଆକର୍ଷଣ କରେ, ତାହାକୁ ସେହି ବସ୍ତୁର ଓଜନ (weight) କୁହାଯାଏ । $W = m \times g$ । ଓଜନ ଏକ ବଳ ହୋଇଥିବାରୁ ଏହା ଏକ ସଦିଶ ରାଶି ଅଟେ ଏବଂ ଏହାର SI ଏକକ ହେଉଛି ନ୍ୟୁଟନ୍ (N) । ଯେହେତୁ ବିଭିନ୍ନ ସ୍ଥାନରେ $g$ ର ମାନ ଭିନ୍ନ ହୁଏ, ତେଣୁ ସ୍ଥାନ ଅନୁସାରେ ବସ୍ତୁର ଓଜନ ବଦଳିଥାଏ ।
- **Glossary Terms**: `T_WEIGHT` (ଓଜନ), `T_ACC_G` (ମାଧ୍ୟାକର୍ଷଣଜନିତ ତ୍ୱରଣ)

### Chunk 3: `BSE9-PHYS-GRAV-S05-03`
- **Title (Odia)**: ଚନ୍ଦ୍ରପୃଷ୍ଠରେ ଓଜନ ଓ ଗାଣିତିକ ତୁଳନା (Weight on the Moon: 1/6th Ratio)
- **Concept ID**: `mass_vs_weight`
- **Source Page**: 101
- **Verbatim Odia Principle**:
  > ଚନ୍ଦ୍ରର ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ଓ ବ୍ୟାସାର୍ଦ୍ଧ ପୃଥିବୀ ତୁଳନାରେ କମ୍ ହୋଇଥିବାରୁ, ଚନ୍ଦ୍ରପୃଷ୍ଠରେ ମାଧ୍ୟାକର୍ଷଣଜନିତ ତ୍ୱରଣ ପୃଥିବୀର ପ୍ରାୟ ଏକ ଷଷ୍ଠାଂଶ ($g_m \approx \frac{1}{6} g_e$) । ଫଳରେ ଚନ୍ଦ୍ରପୃଷ୍ଠରେ ଯେକୌଣସି ବସ୍ତୁର ଓଜନ ପୃଥିବୀ ପୃଷ୍ଠର ଓଜନର $\frac{1}{6}$ ଅଂଶ ହୋଇଥାଏ ($W_m = \frac{1}{6} W_e$) । କିନ୍ତୁ ବସ୍ତୁର ବସ୍ତୁତ୍ୱ ($m$) ଉଭୟ ସ୍ଥାନରେ ସମାନ ରହେ ।
- **Glossary Terms**: `T_MASS`, `T_WEIGHT`

## 4. 3-Way Diagnostic Probe Battery Design (`mass_vs_weight`)

| Modality | Item ID | Item Type | Target Diagnostic Function |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Hero MCQ** | `MVW-HERO` | Initial MCQ | Evaluates basic distinction between mass (kg) and weight (N) on Moon. |
| **P (Picture)** | `MVW-P` | Diagram Probe | Compares beam balance (mass) vs spring balance (weight) on Earth vs Moon. |
| **A (Audio)** | `MVW-A` | Spoken Story | Spoken narration of an Odia astronaut visiting the Moon and feeling lighter. |
| **T (Text)** | `MVW-T` | Symbolic Text | Strict mathematical question: $W = mg$, SI units (kg vs N), scalar vs vector. |
| **Confirmation W** | `MVW-CONF-W` | Worked Example | Step-by-step numeric calculation: 60 kg object weight on Earth (588 N) vs Moon (98 N). |
| **Confirmation TX** | `MVW-CONF-TX` | Reading Comprehension | Text-only passage testing distinction between inertia and gravitational pull. |
| **Confirmation PX** | `MVW-CONF-PX` | Visual Transfer | Identifying balance scale readings in deep space zero-gravity environment. |
