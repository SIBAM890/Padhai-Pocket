# Cross-Subject Extension: The 3-Way Probe (T-A-P) Framework

> **How the Picture (P) · Audio (A) · Text (T) Triangulation Generalizes Across Secondary Science & Mathematics**  
> *BSE Odisha Class 9 Curriculum Mapping*

---

## 1. The Core Pedagogical Principle

The **3-Way Probe (T-A-P)** is not a Physics-specific hack; it is a general diagnostic lens for any secondary subject taught in a language that differs from the learner's mother tongue.

By separating the **pictorial/intuitive representation ($P$)**, the **spoken verbal explanation ($A$)**, and the **written orthographic text ($T$)**, Padhai Pocket isolates whether a student's stumbling block originates in:
1. **Concept Gap ($P=0, A=0, T=0$)**: Fundamental conceptual misunderstanding.
2. **Reading/Decoding Gap ($P=1, A=1, T=0$)**: Student understands the concept and speech, but struggles to decode written technical Odia.
3. **Linguistic Terminology Gap ($P=1, A=0, T=0$)**: Student possesses physical intuition, but confuses formal specialized textbook vocabulary.

## 3. Targeted Confirmation Modalities Across Subjects

For every candidate diagnosis, the engine executes a confirmation step before asserting a diagnosis to the teacher view:

1. **Word-Level Check ($W$)**: Isolates the scientific term from contextual clues (used for `TERM_GAP`).
   * *Physics*: Isolated term card matching *Displacement* (*ବିସ୍ଥାପନ*).
   * *Chemistry*: Isolated term card matching *Physical Change* (*ଭୌତିକ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ*).
   * *Biology*: Isolated term card matching *Cell Wall* (*କୋଷଭିତ୍ତି*).
2. **Text-Reading Check ($TX$)**: Tests sentence-level decoding with spoken audio guidance (used for `READING_GAP`).
   * Read-aloud question with synchronous audio highlighting to verify whether auditory support eliminates errors.
3. **Visual Concept Check ($PX$)**: Tests physical/spatial understanding with zero textual interference (used for `CONCEPT_GAP`).
   * Pure diagrammatic multiple-choice selection to confirm conceptual models.
