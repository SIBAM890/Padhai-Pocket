## 1. Purpose of Recording and Validation
Thank you for participating as a Native Speaker Language Validator for **Padhai Pocket**.  
Padhai Pocket is an offline-first regional education application designed for secondary school students in tribal districts of Odisha who speak Kui at home and study science in Odia at school.  
The purpose of this session is to validate everyday spoken Kui terms, explanations, and short voice prompts that bridge Odia textbook science concepts into natural spoken Kui.

---

## 2. Scope of Use
- **Recordings**: Short spoken audio clips (~2–5 seconds per concept/term) explaining physical science vocabulary in everyday Kui.
- **Transcriptions**: Everyday explanations written down phonetically using standard Odia script (the established written medium for Kui in Odisha MLE).
- **Application**: The audio and text will be bundled directly into the offline Padhai Pocket application prototype demonstrated at NEXTECH 2.0.

---
