# Kui Native Speaker Voice Contribution & Informed Consent

**Project**: Padhai Pocket (Team Coding_Aghoris, NEXTECH 2.0)  
**Field Program**: Offline Multilingual Diagnostic & Remediation Agent for Class 9 Science  

---

### 1. Purpose of the Recording
The purpose of this voice recording is to provide high-quality audio explanations in the **Kui language** for Class 9 science terminology. These recordings serve children from Kui-home backgrounds who are transitioning to Odia-medium science education, helping bridge language and terminology gaps when answering diagnostic science questions.

### 2. Scope of Use
- Voice clips recorded under this consent will be stored on-device within the **Padhai Pocket** application to provide spoken remediation, audio glossaries, and pronunciation support for students.
- Clips are stored in optimized low-bitrate format (Opus ~16 kbps) for offline mobile use.
